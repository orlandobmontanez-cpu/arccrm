The fine print

  • Referrals must be new customers and must name you before their estimate.
  • The homeowner you refer must agree to hear from Agency Roofing Co.
  • One referrer per project. There's no limit on how many neighbors you introduce.
  • Gift cards go out after the inspection or estimate appointment.
  • Cash rewards go out within 14 days of project completion and full payment. Canceled or unpaid projects don't qualify.
  • Rewards never depend on a review.
  • Referrals from insurance, real estate or other licensed professionals are handled separately.
